# Group A guideline-currency audit (conditions 01–05)

Audit date: 2026-11-23. Reference built: 2026-05-11.
Method: each source was checked on the association's own page, or on its PubMed/PMC record (NCBI E-utilities), or on a journal/society summary. When only a secondary summary was available, the item says so. Monograph text was checked against the JSON in the designed_visits page data, because the flattened .txt files lose every `<` character. Several "CURRENT text" quotes below restore those characters.
