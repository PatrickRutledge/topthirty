## 21 Stroke / TIA

### Source status
- ahaasa-stroke-2021 (Kleindorfer 2021 secondary prevention): **CURRENT**. A PubMed search of *Stroke* for guidelines and scientific statements from 06/2025 to 11/2026 found no replacement or focused update. It is now **supplemented** by two new 2026 AHA/ASA guidelines (below).
- aha-pcas-2021 (Kernan 2021 primary care after stroke): **CURRENT**. It is partly overtaken on depression, fall and driving items by the 2026 Rehabilitation guideline (below).
- uspstf-carotid-2021: **CURRENT**. Grade D, 2021-02-02. The USPSTF page shows no update in progress. https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/carotid-artery-stenosis-screening
- acc-arrh-monitor-2024: **CURRENT**. No newer ACC ECDP was found.
- cms-stroke-rehab: **UPDATED**. The CY2026 KX-modifier threshold is $2,480, up from $2,410. https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/therapy-services
- NEW, relevant to monitoring: **ahaasa-rehab-2026**. Richards LG et al. 2026 Guideline for Adult Stroke Rehabilitation and Recovery. Stroke 2026;57(10):e514-e658. It replaces the 2016 rehabilitation guideline. Full text read. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42657476/ ; https://doi.org/10.1161/STR.0000000000000536
- NEW, acute-phase only: **ahaasa-ais-2026**. Prabhakaran S et al. 2026 Guideline for the Early Management of Patients With AIS. Stroke 2026;57(8):e316-e436. It replaces the 2018 guideline and its 2019 update, and covers early secondary prevention. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41582814/
- The ACC/AHA 2017 hypertension guideline quoted in Notes is **SUPERSEDED** by the 2025 AHA/ACC/Multisociety High Blood Pressure Guideline. Circulation 2025;152(11):e114-e218. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40811497/
- The 2018 ACC/AHA Cholesterol guideline quoted in Notes is **SUPERSEDED** by the 2026 ACC/AHA/Multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline (accaha-dyslip-2026, already in the bibliography). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41824552/

### Changes to make
1. **Functional / surveys: depression screen.**
   - CURRENT: "Depression screen (PHQ-9 preferred; PHQ-2 trigger) — at first post-stroke visit and periodically thereafter (at minimum annually)…"
   - NEW: "Depression + anxiety screen (valid instrument, e.g., PHQ-9; adapted / supported-communication tools if aphasic) — early (3 days–3 months post-stroke), again at 3 and 6 months, then periodically lifelong (at minimum annually). Positive screen → comprehensive evaluation by clinician with depression expertise. PHQ-9 ≥10 prompts further evaluation / treatment."
   - Source: ahaasa-rehab-2026 §3.7, recommendations 1–4 (COR 1). This is the more conservative interval, and annual is kept as the floor from aha-pcas-2021.
2. **At-a-glance, Functional row, Annually cell.**
   - CURRENT: "Depression screen (PHQ-9, at minimum annually)"
   - NEW: "Depression + anxiety screen (early, 3 mo, 6 mo, then at minimum annually)"
   - Source: ahaasa-rehab-2026.
3. **Functional / surveys: fall-risk assessment.**
   - CURRENT: "Fall-risk assessment — at every primary-care visit post-stroke."
   - NEW: "Fall-risk query — every visit (Kernan 2021); structured fall-risk evaluation with a validated instrument — at least annually (AHA/ASA 2026, COR 2a). Refer to formal fall-prevention program (COR 1); balance, tai chi or dual-task training (COR 2a); home-modification education for patient + caregiver (COR 1)."
   - Sources: ahaasa-rehab-2026 §3.6; aha-pcas-2021.
4. **Functional / surveys: new item, fracture risk.**
   - NEW: "Fracture-risk assessment (FRAC-Stroke score; ischemic stroke) — at follow-up after ischemic stroke; FRAX does not account for stroke. Counsel aerobic + resistance + balance activity for BMD. (AHA/ASA 2026, COR 2a; no poststroke-specific DXA screening interval exists.)"
   - Source: ahaasa-rehab-2026 §3.8.
5. **Functional / surveys: new item, driving.**
   - NEW: "Return-to-driving evaluation — before resuming driving if cognitive, perceptual or physical impairment; refer for on-road test by authorized evaluator (COR 1)."
   - Source: ahaasa-rehab-2026 §7.8.
6. **Functional / surveys: cognitive screen.**
   - CURRENT: "Cognitive screen (MoCA or MMSE) — before discharge, again during the first year, periodically thereafter."
   - NEW: append "Use a brief multidomain performance-based instrument (cognition + executive function + perception) (COR 1); culturally adapted tool where available; comprehensive evaluation if screen positive."
   - Source: ahaasa-rehab-2026 §4.4.
7. **Functional / surveys: dysphagia screen.**
   - CURRENT: "Dysphagia / swallow screen — before any oral intake post-stroke; reassess if status changes."
   - NEW: "Dysphagia screen — within 24 h of admission and before any oral intake/meds, using a formal validated tool that includes water-swallow trials of varying volume (COR 1); instrumented study (VFSS / FEES) preferred over bedside exam when dysphagia suspected (COR 1); reassess if status changes."
   - Source: ahaasa-rehab-2026, dysphagia section.
8. **Functional / surveys: Medicare outpatient therapy.**
   - CURRENT: "with 2025 threshold of $2,410 for PT+SLP combined and $2,410 for OT before targeted medical review"
   - NEW: "CY2026 KX-modifier threshold $2,480 PT+SLP combined and $2,480 OT (medical-necessity attestation required above it); targeted medical review threshold $3,000 each"
   - Source: cms-therapy-2026. The original wording also conflated the KX threshold with the targeted medical review threshold.
9. **Notes: BP target.**
   - CURRENT: "ACC/AHA 2017 hypertension guideline allows <140/90 mm Hg as fallback when <130/80 is poorly tolerated…"
   - NEW: "2025 AHA/ACC/Multisociety HBP guideline (replaces 2017) sets <130/80 mm Hg for all adults, with individualized considerations (institutional care, limited life expectancy); no routine <140/90 fallback for secondary stroke prevention. AHA/ASA 2021 <130/80 (Class 1) unchanged."
   - Sources: acc-hbp-2025 (https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/articles/2025/10/01/01/new-in-clinical-guidance-hbp) and the PubMed record.
10. **Notes: LDL target.**
    - CURRENT: "2018 ACC/AHA Cholesterol Guideline uses ≥50% LDL reduction with a 'consider <70' threshold…"
    - NEW: "2026 ACC/AHA/Multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline (replaces 2018) restores absolute goals: clinical ASCVD LDL-C <70 / non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL; very-high-risk ASCVD LDL-C <55 / non-HDL-C <85 mg/dL; PCSK9 mAb Class 1 if above goal on max statin; Lp(a) once in lifetime. Concordant with AHA/ASA 2021 <70 for atherosclerotic stroke."
    - Sources: accaha-dyslip-2026 via the ACC feature "Getting to Goals" (https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/articles/2026/09/01/01/feature-getting-to-goals) and the EBSCO summary (https://about.ebsco.com/blogs/health-notes/ebm-focus-top-takeaways-updated-accaha-guideline-management-dyslipidemia).
11. **Laboratory: lipid panel.**
    - CURRENT: "Target LDL-C <70 mg/dL for atherosclerotic stroke / TIA."
    - NEW: "Target LDL-C <70 mg/dL (non-HDL-C <100) for atherosclerotic stroke / TIA; <55 (non-HDL-C <85) if very-high-risk ASCVD (2026 ACC/AHA). Lp(a) — once (if never measured)."
    - Sources: same as change 10. Keep the 4–12 wk then q3–12 mo interval from AHA/ASA 2021 as the more conservative interval.
12. **Summary: add one sentence.**
    - NEW: "The 2026 AHA/ASA Adult Stroke Rehabilitation and Recovery guideline adds longitudinal depression + anxiety screening (early, 3 and 6 months, lifelong), annual validated fall-risk evaluation, FRAC-Stroke fracture-risk assessment, and on-road driving evaluation for impaired patients."
    - Source: ahaasa-rehab-2026.
13. **Primary associations line.**
    - NEW: add "AHA/ASA 2026 Rehabilitation; AHA/ASA 2026 AIS" (the at-a-glance list stays the same otherwise).

### Unverified / flagged
- **DAPT window (2026 AIS guideline).** Secondary snippets suggest three changes: DAPT eligibility now extends to minor stroke with NIHSS ≤5 (previously ≤3); there is a new 24–72 h window (INSPIRES); and there is a CYP2C19-guided ticagrelor branch (CHANCE-2). The class assignments came back garbled. The primary text was 403 and the guidelinecentral page held no text. Do not change the "NIHSS ≤3 … within 24 h" DAPT text until the guideline text is checked: https://doi.org/10.1161/STR.0000000000000513
- **2026 dyslipidemia, very-high-risk definition.** Search summaries describe it as ≥2 major ASCVD events, or 1 major event plus ≥2 high-risk conditions. On that definition, a single ischemic stroke alone would not qualify. This is not confirmed from the primary text.
- **2026 dyslipidemia, re-check interval.** An AFP editorial describes it as "within 12 weeks of initiation and annually thereafter" (https://www.aafp.org/afp/2026/1100/editorials-assessing-acc-aha-dyslipidemia-guideline). If that holds, it conflicts with AHA/ASA 2021 q3–12 mo, and the more conservative q3–12 mo should be kept. Flag the conflict once the primary text is confirmed.
- **Internal error, not a guideline change.** The at-a-glance "As indicated" cell says "INR weekly during DOAC initiation / change". INR is not used to monitor DOACs. Recommend deleting it or replacing it with "Cr/eGFR + CBC at DOAC initiation / dose change".
- **AHA scientific statement on LV systolic dysfunction (2026).** Sharma R et al., Stroke 2026;57(8):e437-e454 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42389837/). It says anticoagulation may have net benefit after stroke with LVEF ≤40% but no thrombus. This is a treatment statement, not a monitoring change. It could optionally go in Notes.
