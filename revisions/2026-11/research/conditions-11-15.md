### Changes to make
1. **Laboratory**, hemoglobin line.
   - CURRENT: "Hemoglobin (CBC) — G3 at least annually; G4–G5 non-dialysis at least twice yearly; G5D every 3 months."
   - NEW: "Anemia panel (CBC, reticulocytes, ferritin, TSAT) — at referral, at least annually G3, at least twice yearly G4, at least every 3 months G5 / G5D; more often on ESA / HIF-PHI, Hb below target, or rapid CKD progression. Anemia = Hb <13.0 g/dL (men), <12.0 g/dL (women)."
   - Source: kdigo-anemia-2026, Practice Point 1.2.1 and Figure 5. The change is that G5 non-dialysis moves from 2×/yr to every 3 months, and reticulocytes, ferritin and TSAT are added to every anemia test.
2. **Laboratory**, iron studies line.
   - CURRENT: "Iron studies (ferritin, TSAT) — at anemia diagnosis and every 3 months during ESA therapy."
   - NEW: "Iron studies (ferritin, TSAT) — with each anemia panel; on iron therapy Hb + ferritin + TSAT every 3 months (non-dialysis / PD) or every 1–3 months (HD); more often after ESA / HIF-PHI start or dose increase, blood loss, or hospitalization. Treat iron deficiency (non-dialysis / PD) if ferritin <100 ng/mL and TSAT <40%, or ferritin 100–<300 with TSAT <25%; hold routine iron if ferritin >700 or TSAT ≥40%."
   - Source: kdigo-anemia-2026, Rec 2.3, PP 2.2, PP 2.5, PP 2.6 (Table 5).
3. **Laboratory**, new line to add after the iron studies line.
   - NEW: "Hb on ESA — every 2–4 weeks after start / dose change (avoid rise >1.0 g/dL per interval); at least every 3 months in maintenance. Target Hb <11.5 g/dL (1D), individualized. On HIF-PHI: Hb 2–4 weeks after start / dose change, then every 4 weeks."
   - Source: kdigo-anemia-2026, Rec 3.3.1, PP 3.3.1, PP 3.4.3.2, PP 3.4.3.3, PP 3.6.1.
4. **Summary**.
   - CURRENT: "Screen hemoglobin annually in G3 and twice yearly in G4–G5 non-dialysis (KDIGO 2012 Anemia in CKD)."
   - NEW: "Screen for anemia (CBC, reticulocytes, ferritin, TSAT) at least annually in G3, twice yearly in G4, and every 3 months in G5 (KDIGO 2026 Anemia in CKD)."
   - Source: kdigo-anemia-2026.
5. **At-a-glance**.
   - Quarterly cell: add "Anemia panel (CBC, retics, ferritin, TSAT) G5 non-dialysis; Hb + ferritin + TSAT on iron therapy."
   - Every 6 months cell: change "Hgb (CBC) for G4–G5 non-dialysis" to "Anemia panel (CBC, retics, ferritin, TSAT) for G4."
   - Annually cell: change "Hgb (CBC) for G3" to "Anemia panel for G3."
   - Source: kdigo-anemia-2026.
6. **Notes**, Hgb target paragraph.
   - CURRENT: "KDIGO 2012 caps ESA-treated Hgb at 11.5 g/dL; the draft KDIGO 2025 / 2026 update adds individualization and HIF-PHI considerations."
   - NEW: "Hgb target in CKD anemia. KDIGO 2026 (final; replaces 2012) keeps ESA-treated Hb <11.5 g/dL (1D) with an individualized target; ESA initiation in non-dialysis CKD is symptom- and risk-based with no fixed Hb trigger; HIF-PHIs are avoided in people at increased risk of adverse events and are never combined with ESAs."
   - Source: kdigo-anemia-2026, Rec 3.2.2, Rec 3.3.1, PP 3.1.3, PP 3.5.1.
7. **Notes**, USPSTF paragraph.
   - CURRENT: "USPSTF maintains an "I" statement for asymptomatic-adult CKD screening"
   - NEW: "USPSTF's 2012 CKD screening statement is now listed as inactive / out of date, and an update is in progress. KDIGO and NKF recommend opportunistic case-finding in diabetes, hypertension, age >60, CVD, and family history. The 2025 AHA/ACC BP guideline adds UACR testing for all patients with hypertension."
   - Sources: USPSTF CKD page (URL above); 2025 AHA/ACC HBP guideline (ACC journal scan URL above).

I could not open the guideline text (AHA returned 403; PubMed needs cookies), so this is not added. If confirmed, it narrows the KDIGO-vs-ACC/AHA conflict in the BP Note.
