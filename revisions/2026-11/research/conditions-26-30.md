# Group F — Guideline currency audit (conditions 26–30)

Audit date: 2026-11-23. Reference build: 2026-05-11.
Method: association pages, NCCN guideline-detail pages (version strings fetched 2026-11-23), PubMed/Crossref records, CDC/CMS/USPSTF/FDA pages. NCCN, AUA, SGO, and JNCCN full texts are mostly behind logins. Where only the version number or abstract could be confirmed, content-level claims are listed under "Unverified".

**Cross-cutting federal item. It affects #26 and every monograph that carries ACIP vaccine lines.**
- On 2026-03-16 the D. Mass. court in *AAP v. Kennedy* stayed the 13 ACIP appointments made in June 2025 and every ACIP vote after 2025-06-11. The stay "mostly revert[s] the childhood and adult immunization schedules to the 2025 versions." The April 2025 ACIP votes remain in effect, including RSV for ages 50–59 at increased risk (CRS IN12684, 2026-04-28).
  - PDF: https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IN/PDF/IN12684/IN12684.1.pdf
- The government appealed (1st Cir. No. 26-1503), and oral argument was reported for 2026-10-06. I found no appellate decision as of 2026-11-23.
- Effect on adults: the September 2025 vote moving COVID-19 vaccination to shared clinical decision-making (SCDM) is **not in effect**. The June 2025 thimerosal-only influenza vote is also not in effect.
- CDC's current adult schedule is the "Adult Immunization Schedule by Age (Addendum updated July 2, 2025)."
  - Schedule: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/adult-age.html
  - Notes: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/adult-notes.html
- CDC's 2026–27 COVID page states: "A 2026-2027 COVID-19 vaccine is recommended for all adults ages 18 years and older." It adds that, "because of legal uncertainties," the July 2025 schedule recommendations remain in effect.
  - https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/covid-19/clinical-considerations/covid-19-vaccines-us.html
